# Overview

> Add context that the original event does not contain.

Enrichment adds context such as asset ownership, threat intelligence, and model-generated labels to events as they pass through a pipeline.

Use [lookup tables](enrich/use-lookup-tables.md), [asset inventory](enrich/enrich-with-asset-inventory.md), [threat intelligence](enrich/enrich-with-threat-intel.md), or [AI enrichment](enrich/enrich-events-with-ai.md) to supply the context your downstream systems need.
